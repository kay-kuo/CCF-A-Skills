# CCF-A Writing Style Guide

## Preferred style

- Precise, technical, and evidence-bound.
- Use explicit problem and challenge statements.
- Contributions should be concrete and falsifiable.
- Avoid marketing language such as "revolutionary", "unprecedented", "game-changing".
- Avoid vague novelty claims such as "to the best of our knowledge" unless backed by related-work analysis.

## Claim strength ladder

Strong:
- "outperforms X under Y setting by Z"
- "reduces error on dataset D under metric M"

Moderate:
- "improves robustness under the evaluated shifts"
- "provides evidence that ..."

Weak/exploratory:
- "suggests a potential direction"
- "may benefit settings where ..."

## Common CCF-A paragraph pattern

```text
Problem. Existing methods ...
Challenge. However, ...
Insight. We observe that ...
Method. Based on this insight, we propose ...
Evidence. Experiments on ... show ...
Boundary. The method is limited by ...
```
